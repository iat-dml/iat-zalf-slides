# PB3 data catalogue

The data catalogue is a service of the DML working group (Data and Modelling
Infrastructure for Living Labs) at the IAT.

- Went live in May 2026. It indexes datasets from the three IAT living labs.
- 212 datasets registered by 1 September 2026, from 31 projects.
- 14 of the 31 projects still have no data management plan; their datasets are
  registered with minimal metadata only.
- Metadata follow the ZALF research data policy; persistent identifiers are
  assigned through the ZALF repository.
- Next steps: metadata review with project leads in autumn 2026, link to the
  IAT project tracker, training for new projects.
- Open question for the seminar: who signs off metadata quality per living lab?
